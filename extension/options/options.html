yt-dlp Downloader

Each right-click action, Download Video and Download Audio, has its own defaults.

Download Video

Resolution is the highest you want; if a video has nothing that low, the smallest size above is used.

Empty = host default. Absolute path or ~/…; saved on Enter or when you leave the field.

Download Audio

Quality applies only when the audio is converted. If YouTube already serves the preferred format, or the format is Original, the stream is saved as-is.

Empty = host default. Absolute path or ~/…; saved on Enter or when you leave the field.
